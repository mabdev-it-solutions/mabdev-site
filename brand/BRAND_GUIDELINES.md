## 2. Visual Identity

### 2.1 Logo

The Mabdev logo consists of two elements:

1. **Logomark:** A geometric, isometric "M" constructed from 3D blocks
2. **Logotype:** "MABDEV" in a clean sans-serif typeface with a stylized "E"

**Logo Characteristics:**
- Modern and technical, suggesting precision and structure
- Isometric 3D effect conveys depth and dimensionality
- Clean lines represent clarity and professionalism

**Logo Versions:**
- **Primary:** White logo on dark background (preferred)
- **Inverted:** Dark logo on light background
- **Monochrome:** Single color applications

**Clear Space:**
Maintain minimum clear space equal to the height of the "M" logomark on all sides.

**Minimum Size:**
- Digital: 32px height minimum
- Print: 12mm height minimum

## 3. Voice & Tone

### 3.1 Brand Voice

**Professional but Warm**

Mabdev communicates like a senior colleague you trust—knowledgeable and confident, but approachable and genuinely helpful. Never stiff or corporate, never too casual or unprofessional.

### 3.2 Voice Attributes

| Attribute | Description | Example |
|-----------|-------------|---------|
| **Confident** | Speaks with authority earned through experience | "With 15+ years building e-commerce solutions, I understand the challenges you're facing." |
| **Clear** | Explains complex topics simply | "Your warehouse system will talk directly to your e-commerce platform—no manual exports." |
| **Genuine** | Honest and direct, no marketing fluff | "This approach works best for businesses processing 1,000+ orders monthly." |
| **Supportive** | Focused on client success | "Let's find the right solution for your specific situation." |

### 3.3 Tone Variations

| Context | Tone Adjustment |
|---------|-----------------|
| **Website Homepage** | Confident, welcoming, benefit-focused |
| **Technical Documentation** | Clear, precise, helpful |
| **Case Studies** | Results-focused, narrative, credible |
| **Email Communication** | Warm, direct, action-oriented |
| **Error Messages** | Calm, helpful, solution-oriented |

### 3.4 Writing Guidelines

**Do:**
- Use "I" when speaking as Vinicius, "we" when speaking as Mabdev generally
- Lead with benefits, follow with features
- Use concrete numbers and specifics
- Write in active voice
- Keep sentences concise

**Don't:**
- Use jargon without explanation
- Make vague claims ("best-in-class," "cutting-edge")
- Over-promise or use superlatives
- Be self-deprecating or overly humble
- Use emojis in professional content

### 3.5 Messaging Examples

**Instead of:** "We leverage synergistic methodologies to optimize your digital transformation journey."

**Write:** "I'll help you modernize your systems—step by step, with minimal disruption to your daily operations."

---

**Instead of:** "Our team of experts delivers world-class solutions."

**Write:** "With 15+ years of hands-on experience, I bring senior-level expertise to every project."

## 7. Website Direction

### 7.1 Design Philosophy

**Inspired by:** Linear

**Aesthetic:** Modern tech startup with dark mode as primary, minimal and focused, subtle sophistication through micro-interactions and thoughtful details.

### 7.2 Key Design Principles

1. **Dark Mode First:** Charcoal backgrounds with white text as the default experience
2. **Generous Whitespace:** Let content breathe; don't crowd elements
3. **Subtle Depth:** Use shadows, gradients, and glassmorphism sparingly but effectively
4. **Purposeful Animation:** Smooth transitions and micro-interactions that feel polished, not flashy
5. **Typography-Led:** Strong typographic hierarchy; let the words do the work

### 7.3 Visual Elements

#### Gradients
- Use subtle amber-to-transparent gradients for accent areas
- Dark gradient overlays on hero sections
- Avoid rainbow or multi-color gradients

#### Glassmorphism
- Apply to cards, modals, and floating elements
- Use backdrop-blur with semi-transparent backgrounds
- Keep effect subtle (blur: 8-16px, opacity: 80-90%)

#### Borders & Dividers
- Use subtle borders (1px, low opacity white or gray)
- Glowing borders on hover for interactive elements
- Gradient borders for premium feel on key elements

#### Grid & Layout
- Use grid patterns as subtle background texture
- Consistent spacing system (8px base)
- Maximum content width: 1200-1400px

### 7.4 Page Structure Recommendations

#### Homepage
1. **Hero:** Bold headline, sub-headline, single CTA
2. **Trust Bar:** Years of experience, technologies, client count (if applicable)
3. **Services Overview:** 3-4 cards highlighting key services
4. **About Snippet:** Brief intro to Vinicius with link to full bio
5. **Featured Work:** 2-3 case study previews
6. **CTA Section:** Clear call to action to get in touch

#### About Page
1. **Hero:** Meet the expert behind Mabdev
2. **Full Bio:** Vinicius's story and experience
3. **Expertise Areas:** Technical skills and specializations
4. **Values:** Quality & Excellence, Client Success
5. **CTA:** Let's work together

#### Services Page(s)
1. **Overview:** All services with brief descriptions
2. **Individual Service Pages:** Deep dive into each capability
3. **Process:** How engagements typically work
4. **CTA:** Discuss your project

#### Work/Portfolio Page
1. **Featured Case Studies:** Detailed project stories
2. **Project Grid:** Visual overview of work
3. **Technologies Used:** Tech stack showcase
4. **Results:** Measurable outcomes when possible

#### Contact Page
1. **Personal Message:** From Vinicius
2. **Contact Form:** Simple, focused
3. **Alternative Contact:** Email as backup
4. **Response Expectation:** Set timing expectations

### 7.5 Interactive Elements

**Buttons:**
- Primary: Amber background, dark text
- Secondary: Transparent with amber border
- Ghost: Text-only with underline on hover

**Links:**
- Amber color with subtle underline on hover
- External links: Include subtle icon indicator

**Cards:**
- Glassmorphism effect on hover
- Subtle border glow on interaction
- Smooth scale transform (1.02) on hover

**Form Fields:**
- Dark backgrounds with subtle borders
- Amber focus ring
- Clear validation states

### 7.6 Responsive Considerations

- Mobile-first development approach
- Maintain visual hierarchy at all breakpoints
- Touch-friendly tap targets (min 44px)
- Simplified navigation for mobile
